OUR PARISH

A Parish of Faith Since 1935

Lourde Matha Church is a Catholic parish at Thalayanadu, Idukki — serving the faithful under the patronage of Our Lady of Lourdes since 26 October 1935.

Rooted in the Catholic faith and guided by the teachings of the Church, our parish gathers daily for Holy Mass and prayer, celebrates the sacraments of Christian life, and reaches out in charity to all. For over ninety years, generation after generation of the faithful of Thalayanadu have called this church their spiritual home.

Our Patroness

Our Lady of Lourdes

✦ ✦ ✦

Our parish stands under the loving patronage of Our Lady of Lourdes.

Since 1935, the faithful of Thalayanadu have gathered before their Mother — in joy and in sorrow, in gratitude and in need. Her grotto in our parish remains a place of quiet prayer, where generations have lit a candle and found comfort in her intercession.

We celebrate our parish feast in her honour every February (Feast of Our Lady of Lourdes — 11 February), and gather for her novena on the 11th of every month at 5:30 PM.

Message from Fr. Vicar

V. Rev. Fr Alex Thannikunnel

Vicar

Dear brothers and sisters in Christ, welcome to Lourde Matha Church, Thalayanadu. For ninety years, this parish has been a home of prayer for the families of this region, gathered around the Holy Eucharist and under the loving protection of Our Lady of Lourdes.

Whether you are a parishioner or a visitor passing through, you are welcome here — to pray, to find peace, and to grow in faith together as one parish family. May Our Lady of Lourdes intercede for you and your loved ones, and may the Lord bless you abundantly.

Our Heritage

✦ ✦ ✦

Lourde Matha Church was established on 26 October 1935 at Thalayanadu, on the Kolapra–Thalayanadu Road in Idukki district, Kerala. From a humble beginning, the parish has grown into a vibrant spiritual home for the Catholic families of the region.

Through nine decades — through times of joy and trial — the parish has remained a beacon of faith, with the Holy Eucharist at its heart and Our Lady of Lourdes as its heavenly patroness.
