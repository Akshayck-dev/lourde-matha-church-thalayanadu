Welcome to Lourde Matha Church

✝ Nine Decades of Faith in Thalayanadu

A Catholic parish under the patronage of Our Lady of Lourdes
✦ ✦ ✦

Established on 26 October 1935, Lourde Matha Church stands on the Kolapra–Thalayanadu Road in the heart of Idukki district. For over ninety years, this parish has been the spiritual home of the Catholic families of Thalayanadu — a place where faith is handed down from generation to generation, and where all are welcome to pray.

Rooted in the faith and traditions of the Catholic Church since 1935.
