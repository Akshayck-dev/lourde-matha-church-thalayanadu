Welcome to Lourde Matha Church

✝ Nine Decades of Faith in Thalayanadu

A Catholic parish under the patronage of Our Lady of Lourdes
✦ ✦ ✦

Established on 26 October 1935, Lourde Matha Church stands on the Kolapra–Thalayanadu Road in the heart of Idukki district. For over ninety years, this parish has been the spiritual home of the Catholic families of Thalayanadu — a place where faith is handed down from generation to generation, and where all are welcome to pray.

Rooted in the faith and traditions of the Catholic Church since 1935.

Message from Fr. Vicar

— ✝ —
V. Rev. Fr Alex Thannikunnel

Vicar

“

Dear brothers and sisters in Christ, welcome to Lourde Matha Church, Thalayanadu. For ninety years, this parish has been a home of prayer for the families of this region, gathered around the Holy Eucharist and under the loving protection of Our Lady of Lourdes.

Whether you are a parishioner or a visitor passing through, you are welcome here — to pray, to find peace, and to grow in faith together as one parish family. May Our Lady of Lourdes intercede for you and your loved ones, and may the Lord bless you abundantly.

”
